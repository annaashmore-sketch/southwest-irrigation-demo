FAQ

Common questions

What warranty do you offer?

Every new installation comes with a 3-year written warranty, free adjustments for the first 30 days, and our 100% satisfaction guarantee.

Are your estimates free?

Yes, estimates for sprinkler, drainage, and lighting installations are free. (Service repairs are scheduled separately.)

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Licensed Irrigator LI0009024 and Backflow Prevention Assembly Tester BP0014356. All irrigators and technicians are licensed with the State of Texas, and we carry liability and workers' compensation insurance.

What are your hours?

Our office is open Monday to Friday 8am to 5pm and Saturday 8am to 4pm. Our online helper answers questions and takes requests 24/7.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes. Seasonal check-ups keep your system working year-round, and VIP membership plans start at $20 a month.

Will drain cleaning damage my pipes?

No. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water only. It's safe for black corrugated pipe and won't tear it up the way a roto-rooter can.
